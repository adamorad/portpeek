Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

PortPeek is a Mac menu bar app and MCP server that helps developers manage local network ports. This policy covers both the PortPeek app and this website.

The PortPeek App

The PortPeek app and its MCP server operate entirely on your device. They scan ports on localhost only and do not transmit any data — including port numbers, process names, or usage patterns — to any external server. No account is required to use the app.

This Website

This website offers an optional email notification form for people who want to be notified when PortPeek launches on the Mac App Store. If you submit your email address:

— Your email is sent to FormSubmit.co, a third-party form service, and forwarded to the developer. It is used only to send a one-time launch notification and is never sold or shared with other parties.

— You can request deletion of your email at any time by emailing adammor17@gmail.com.

Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Children's Privacy

PortPeek is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect that.
